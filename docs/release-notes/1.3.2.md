**What's new in 1.3.2**

- **A cut-off answer no longer passes for a finished one.** When an answer hits its length limit, loses its connection, or you press Stop, the seat says INCOMPLETE and one line under the answer says why. The mark is still there when you reopen the session, and it goes into exports, the decision memo and the CLI.
- **Half an answer doesn't get a vote.** An incomplete answer stays out of peer review, the divergence score and the dissent unless you bring it back in, and it isn't replayed to the model on your next question. If only one answer finished, Council says so instead of scoring it.
- **Reviewers read what you asked.** Blind peer review now sees the question, the seat's persona and an attached document of up to 20,000 characters, so a critique can say an answer missed the point. That makes each review a bigger request (up to about 5,000 more input tokens), and a small local model may not have room for it. A longer document, or an image, isn't shown to reviewers, and they are told so.
- **The synthesis stops picking a winner.** It says what the council settled, what you still have to decide, and where they split. The decision stays yours.
- **A model that is thinking is not a model that hung.** Most current models think before their first word. Council used to give up on a seat after 90 seconds without answer text; now anything the provider sends while the model works counts as a sign of life, for up to ten minutes without a word of the answer. A provider that sends nothing at all while its model thinks can still time out.
- **Model lists are current again.** DeepSeek retired `deepseek-chat` and `deepseek-reasoner` in July, so its default is now `deepseek-flash`. A seat still set to a retired name (those two, the old Grok fast models, `sonar-reasoning`, `gemini-3.5-flash`) moves to its replacement on its own.
- **CLI:** two seats on the same provider are told apart (`Ollama (1)`, `Ollama (2)`) and `--deliberate` can name either, a stage that failed or was cut off is reported, and the JSON carries each answer's status and the model that wrote it. `council.cli.v1` only gains fields.
- **Fixes:** Stop used to save a half-written answer as a whole one. A stream that died partway was recorded as done (now caught for Claude, OpenAI, Gemini and OpenRouter, and for any server that reports the failure itself). Mistral Large answers keep their first words (it thinks first now, and sends that in a different shape). Claude's newer models and GPT-6 no longer fail when a seat has a temperature set (they don't take one, so Council leaves it out). A Claude seat with no cap can write up to 16,000 tokens instead of 8,192, because the newer models count their thinking against it. In the one-page layout, a divergence or synthesis that failed after peer review used to leave nothing on screen and no way to run it again; now the stage says why, and RUN on the analysis card picks up whatever is missing.

Official downloads come only from this repo's Releases and the `albertofettucini/council` Homebrew tap.

After updating, Council asks you to re-enter your API keys once. Every unsigned build is a new app to macOS; your keys never leave the Keychain.
