**What's new in 1.3.1**

- **See a full council before adding a key.** Open the example council from the welcome card, or from Home until your first session: a real recorded session with answers, blind review, a divergence score and the dissent. It's read-only and never lands in your history.
- **One answer is not a council, and now Council says so.** When only one advisor answered, a quiet card explains why and offers a second view: ask another connected model in the same round, add a free local one, or paste another AI's answer (ChatGPT's, Claude's, Gemini's) into that round, where it's reviewed blind like any advisor.
- **Fixes:** Save PDF makes a readable, paginated document; reopened sessions name the provider that actually wrote each answer; CLI runs and the last edit before quitting now always reach your history.

Official downloads come only from this repo's Releases and the `albertofettucini/council` Homebrew tap.

After updating, Council asks you to re-enter your API keys once. Every unsigned build is a new app to macOS; your keys never leave the Keychain.
